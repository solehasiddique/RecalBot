RecallBot is a student-focused research project tackling one of education's biggest challenges - forgetting what we study. By combining AI intelligence with proven learning techniques like spaced repetition and active recall, RecallBot creates a smarter, personalized way to study. Built for academic research, it bridges the gap between how students study and how the brain truly remembers.
